Everything you need, nothing you don't

A complete texture painting toolset without the bloat of a full DCC suite.

  • Direct 3D Surface PaintingBrush is a 3D projector, never bleeds through thin walls or paints hidden geometry.
  • Non-Destructive LayersHide, reorder, adjust opacity, duplicate, and merge with live composite blending.
  • Effects BrushBlur, sharpen, smudge, and pixelate existing paint. Four modes, independent per-mode controls.
  • Screen-Space StencilLoad a PNG that floats on the viewport (Mari/Mudbox workflow). Paint through it or stamp as a decal.
  • Pen Pressure & Line SnappingStylus pressure controls size and opacity independently. Shift+click draws straight lines on curved geometry.
  • Edge Wear & Crevice DirtTwo procedural generators with live preview. Wear highlights ridges; Crevice fills interior folds with grime.
  • Face Selection & MaskingHold Ctrl on any tool to select faces and confine strokes. Layer masks multiply below with explicit stacking.
  • Symmetry Mirror PaintingMirror every stroke, stamp, and fill across the model's X, Y, or Z axis in real time.
  • One-Click ExportSave composite PNGs ready for Godot, Unity, Unreal, Blender, or any game engine.
