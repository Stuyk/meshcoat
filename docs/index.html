Everything you need, nothing you don't

A complete texture painting toolset without the bloat of a full DCC suite.

  • Direct 3D Surface PaintingStrokes and patterns project straight onto your model's UV layout.
  • Non-Destructive LayersHide, reorder, adjust opacity, duplicate, and merge with live blending.
  • Full Tool SuiteBrush, stamp, eraser, fill bucket, eyedropper, and face selection.
  • Quick Face MaskingHold Ctrl on any tool to confine strokes and fills to selected faces.
  • Integrated Texture ShelfBrowse local folders of PNG/JPG textures with instant search.
  • Edge Wear WizardProcedural ridge, chip, and corrosion generation on mesh edges.
  • Lighting & WireframeToggle lit or flat shading, and overlay wireframe to inspect UVs.
  • Format SupportLoads .obj, .gltf, and .glb with automatic UV validation.
  • One-Click ExportExport composite PNGs ready for Godot, Unity, Unreal, or Blender.
